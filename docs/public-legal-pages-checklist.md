# Public Legal Pages Checklist

> This document is an engineering compliance checklist, not legal advice.
> Before public release, paid distribution, user-data collection, or production operation, confirm the final position with a qualified professional.


## Scope

This repository hosts public support and privacy pages for GratefulMoments.

## Checks

- Privacy page matches the app implementation and App Store privacy labels.
- Support page warns users not to email sensitive journal content or payment details.
- Premium wording defers payment method management to Apple where appropriate.
- English and Japanese pages are kept in sync if both markets are targeted.

## Recommended Additions

- Japanese Privacy Policy page.
- Last-updated date on support and privacy pages.
- Link back to Ether LLC website legal portal if one is created.
